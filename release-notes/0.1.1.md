Steadier and easier to install.
- New one-line installer for Terminal. It puts Perch in your own Applications folder when your account cannot use the main one, and clears out stuck or old copies first.
- Perch finishes opening before it asks about moving, so the Dock icon no longer bounces while it waits.
- The resting island stays perfectly still: no breathing dots or moving bars beside the cover.
- After the island closes it waits for the pointer to leave before it can open again, so it never flaps open and shut.
