Perch, rebuilt.
- Seven tabs: Today, Music, Notes, Agents, Shelf, Mail and Launch. Hide the ones you never use.
- Today puts the date, weather, what is playing, your next class, a focus timer, your agents and homework due in one glance.
- Music has big art over its own glow, a scrub bar, shuffle and repeat, volume, synced lyrics and a Spotify or Music switch.
- Moments: the island widens for volume and brightness, the charger, AirPods, the camera turning on, new mail, a class starting, a timer ending and an agent finishing.
- The wings beside the cover show what matters most right now: camera, timer, next class, agents, music or charging.
- Notes read like notes now, with headings, bold and boxes you can tick. Homework by class, with due dates typed in plain words.
- Agents get their own avatar and color, a live clock, and a history kept on your Mac.
- The Shelf holds files you drag up to the island, with AirDrop, plus clipboard history that skips passwords.
- Real Gmail with your own Google sign-in, kept in your Keychain.
- Launch your apps and Shortcuts in one click.
- Ink, Graphite and Paper themes, an accent of your choice, a keyboard shortcut (Control Option Space) and swipe down to open.
- Every permission is asked once, only when you press a button.
