# Perch 0.3.0: Liquid

Perch is now a drop of ink hanging from the top of your screen.

## Pour

Everything in Perch is now a droplet you can pick up and pour somewhere else.

- Drag a note, a to-do, a song, a lyric, a clip, an email or a file out of the island. Drop it on another tab, or on any other app.
- Drop on Notes to make a note, on Shelf to keep it (links and text become files you can drag out later), on Mail to start an email.
- Drop on Agents to hand it to your Grok Bots. It goes into a queue on your agent board that they can read and pick up. Files stay on your Mac; agents get the name, and the text inside if it is a small text file.
- Drag text or a link up from any app and the island opens and splits it into droplets: Note, To-do, Shelf, Agent, Email or Copy.
- The tab selection is a bead of liquid that stretches to the next tab and snaps together there. Tabs swell when you hold something over them.
- Flick down hard on the top edge to spill the island into a wider workspace. The pool appears beside the pane: everything that matters right now, heaviest first. Late homework and the next class sink to the front, and finished things evaporate. Flick up to gather it again.
- A one-time hint in the footer shows how.

## Liquid

- The island is drawn as liquid. It stretches out of the notch, overshoots a little and settles, and draws back up when it closes. A thin bright edge catches the light like the surface of water.
- Wings bud out of the pill like droplets, with a soft waist where they meet it.
- When an agent finishes, ink unfurls in the wing and a bead swells under it, pinches off and falls.
- Opening by hover or click sends a ripple out from your pointer. Buttons squeeze like a drop when pressed.
- Tabs flow in behind a soft wave front.
- The song's color seeps into the music pane like dye.
- Volume and brightness are liquid in a tube that sloshes past where it lands. Charging fills the battery like a glass of water.
- New sounds made for Perch: a drop of water when an agent finishes, a lower one if it stopped. Optional water sounds for opening and plugging in (off by default).
- Themes reworked: Sumi ink, Deep water and Washi paper. Accents: Koi, Tide and Indigo, plus the cover lime and dye from album art.
- Still completely still at rest. Nothing moves unless something happens.
- Turn off the liquid surface in Settings > Look. It also switches to the plain shape by itself in Low Power Mode.
